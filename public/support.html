Macrovana Support

Get help with meal logging, your account, connected health data, or the Macrovana iPhone app.

Contact us

Email info@macrovana.com. Include your app version, a description of the problem, and any request reference shown in an error message. Please do not send passwords or private health records.

Signing in

Use Continue with Apple or Continue with Google. Use the same sign-in account each time to access your existing history.

Apple Health and Oura

Connections are optional. In Settings, review each source's Read and Write choices and its latest sync status. Apple Health also requires permission in Apple's Health settings. You can disconnect Oura and delete its imported data from Settings.

Correcting nutrition estimates

Review calories and macros before saving AI or barcode estimates. You can edit saved meal entries when a portion or nutrition value needs correcting.

Export or delete your account

Open Settings in the iPhone app, or Account & Privacy on the website, to export your account data or delete your account. Individual progress photos can be exported from their check-in.
